How XP Works

XP is earned only from running shows and hitting first-time milestones. There is no passive XP gain.

  • Show base: 50 XP
  • Per star rating: 20 XP
  • Sellout bonus: 100 XP
  • Per 500 attendance: 8 XP
  • 5-star match: 200 XP each
  • 4.5-star match: 100 XP each
  • 4-star match: 50 XP each
  • PPV multipliers: All values doubled
📋 Booking Shows

The Booking Room App

Open the Booking Room app from your phone home screen to access:

  • Match Booking: Build your card
  • Production & Logistics: Setup show production
  • Venue Hire: Choose your venue
  • Tournaments: Coming soon
  • Skip Week: Advance time
  • Run Show: Execute booked show

Booking Workflow

  1. Pick a date from the Calendar
  2. Open Booking Room → Match Booking
  3. Select a venue (check perks/restrictions)
  4. Add matches (drag to reorder, last = Main Event)
  5. Set match time and rules per match
  6. Setup Production for quality bonuses
  7. Book Show or Run Now

Venue Perks & Restrictions

Each venue has unique perks (alcohol sales, VIP areas, pyro allowed) and restrictions (no hardcore, no blood, noise curfew). Check before booking!

Day-of-Week matters: Saturday shows draw bigger crowds but cost more. Some bars do better on Fridays. Community centers shine on Sundays.

Time Limits & Overruns

Every venue has a maximum show duration. Going over results in:

  • 5+ min over: Small fine, prestige loss
  • 10+ min over: Bigger fine, venue trust drops
  • 15+ min over: Severe fine + 4-week venue ban
  • 25+ min over: Catastrophic — major fines + 12-week ban

🏆 Championships

Creating Titles

Unlock championship slots first ($10K, $25K, $50K, etc.), then create titles:

  • World Championship: $25,000 — Top prize
  • Singles Championship: $15,000 — Midcard title
  • Tag Team Championship: $12,000 — For tag teams (2 champs)
  • Trophy/Special: $5,000 — Special accolades

Championship Rules

  • Standard: Defend in any match type
  • Hardcore Only: Hardcore/deathmatch defenses only
  • Iron Man Only: Iron Man matches only
  • Submission Only: Win by submission/I Quit
  • Ladder Match Only: Ladder/TLC defenses
  • Tournament Only: New champs through tournaments
💎 Prestige System

What is Prestige?

Prestige is a 1-100 score representing how important and valuable a championship is. The WWE Championship has high prestige; a meaningless midcard belt has low prestige.

How Prestige Changes

ActionEffect
Title awarded to new champion+2
Title successfully defended+1
Title vacated-10
Sitting vacant (per week)-1
Champion not defending 8+ weeks-1/week

Why Prestige Matters

  • Match Quality: Higher prestige = bigger crowd reactions
  • Title Match Bonuses: +0.2 rating bonus to high-prestige matches
  • Wrestler Morale: Holding a prestigious title boosts morale
  • Fan Investment: Higher prestige = fans care more
  • Storyline Weight: Affects future rivalry/storyline systems

Starting Prestige by Tier

  • 🏆 World Championship: 60
  • 🥇 Singles Championship: 45
  • 🤝 Tag Team Championship: 40
  • 🏅 Trophy/Special: 30

💡 Long, well-defended reigns build prestige. Hot potato title changes destroy it.

Promotion Prestige

Your promotion's prestige is separate from championship prestige. It affects venue attendance, fan growth, and overall reputation. Increases through:

  • Running successful shows
  • 5-star matches
  • Sellout crowds
  • Major events (Mania Weekend, etc.)

Decreases from: bad shows, scandals, venue overruns, defaults on loans.

🏥 Injury Report

How Injuries Work

Wrestlers can get injured during matches. Injury severity ranges from minor bruises to career-threatening neck injuries. Access the Injury Report from the Locker Room app.

Severity Levels

  • Minor: 3-21 days recovery (bruises, sprains)
  • Moderate: 21-45 days (concussion, dislocations)
  • Serious: 45-90 days (torn muscles, broken bones)
  • Severe: 90-180 days (torn ACL, herniated disc)
  • Career Threatening: 180+ days (neck injuries)

Surgery Decisions

Some injuries require surgery. You have three choices:

  • 💰 Promotion Pays: Faster recovery (-30%), wrestler morale +15
  • 🤼 Wrestler Pays: Faster recovery, wrestler morale -20
  • ❌ Decline Surgery: 50% longer recovery, morale -5, reinjury risk

💡 Better medical production reduces injury severity AND speeds recovery.

💡 Pro Tips

Early Game Strategy

  • Sign 4-6 wrestlers immediately after accepting your origin grant
  • Start with backyard venues — they're cheap and you keep more profit
  • Run shows on Saturdays for the attendance bonus
  • Match the time to wrestler skill (Standard 12 min is safest early)
  • Don't take loans early — interest compounds quickly

Mid Game Strategy

  • Create your first championship around level 5
  • Build long title reigns with defenses to grow prestige
  • Invest in better Medical production to reduce injuries
  • Save Saturday slots for PPVs once unlocked at level 21
  • Diversify your roster — different styles for different matches

Booking Wisdom

  • Match opposite alignments (face vs heel) for chemistry bonuses
  • Don't rematch the same wrestlers every week
  • Push popular wrestlers to higher card positions
  • Use Iron Man matches for technician championships
  • Save hardcore matches for venues that allow them

Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Taking out a Loan Shark loan early
  • ❌ Booking Broadway matches with rookie wrestlers
  • ❌ Vacating titles unnecessarily (-10 prestige!)
  • ❌ Overrunning the venue time limit
  • ❌ Declining surgery to save money (causes long-term morale issues)
  • ❌ Booking hardcore matches at family-friendly venues
