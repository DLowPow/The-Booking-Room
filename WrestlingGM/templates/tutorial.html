📚 Tutorial & Guide

Everything you need to know about running your wrestling promotion.

Getting Started Wrestler Stats Booking Shows Production Championships Progression Creative Control Tips & Tricks

🎬 Getting Started

Welcome to The Booking Room! You are the General Manager of a wrestling promotion. Your job is to sign talent, book shows, create championships, and grow your promotion from a backyard operation to a global empire.

Your First Steps

  • Sign Wrestlers - Go to Free Agents and sign your first 3-5 wrestlers. Look for affordable talent with decent stats.
  • Book a Show - Select a venue, set up production, add matches, and run your first show.
  • Earn XP & Money - Running shows earns you XP to level up and money to grow.
  • Advance Weeks - Running a show automatically advances to the next week.
  • Grow Your Promotion - Level up to unlock bigger venues, more roster slots, and new match types.
💡 Tip: Don't overspend early! Start with cheap wrestlers and small venues. Build slowly.

📊 Wrestler Stats Explained

Every wrestler has stats that affect their performance in matches and your shows.

Core Stats (Visible)

⭐ OVR (Overall Rating)

The average of all core stats. Higher overall means a better all-round performer. Ranges from 1-100.

📈 POP (Popularity)

How well-known and liked the wrestler is by fans. Higher popularity draws bigger crowds and sells more merchandise. Affects attendance and fan growth.

💪 Power

Physical strength. Affects power moves like slams, suplexes, and powerbombs. Powerhouse and Giant styles benefit most from high power.

⚡ Speed

Quickness and agility. Affects high-flying moves, dodging, and pace of matches. High Flyers and Luchadors need high speed.

🔧 Technical

Wrestling ability and mat skills. Affects holds, reversals, and chain wrestling. Technicians and Submission Artists excel with high technical.

❤️ Stamina

Endurance and conditioning. Affects how long a wrestler can perform at peak level. Important for Iron Man matches and long shows.

🎤 Charisma

Crowd connection and mic skills. Affects crowd reactions, promo quality, and storyline engagement. Showmen need high charisma.

✈️ Aerial

High-flying ability. Affects top rope moves, dives, and spectacular spots. Essential for High Flyers and Luchadors.

🩸 Hardcore

Ability to work hardcore/extreme matches. Affects weapon use, bleeding, and deathmatch performance. Brawlers and Hardcore specialists need this.

Status Stats

🔥 Momentum

Current hot streak or cold streak. Winning builds momentum, losing drops it. High momentum means better performances and crowd reactions. Ranges 0-100.

😊 Morale

How happy the wrestler is. Affected by wins, losses, pay, and how you treat them. Low morale can cause problems especially with Creative Control on.

😫 Fatigue

How tired the wrestler is. Builds up from matches and resets over time with rest. High fatigue means worse performances and higher injury risk.

Hidden Stats

These stats are not shown directly but affect behavior behind the scenes:

🎯 Consistency

How reliably the wrestler performs at their best. High consistency means fewer bad nights.

💼 Professionalism

How professional and reliable the wrestler is. Low professionalism can lead to no-shows, drama, and going into business for themselves.

🤝 Loyalty

How loyal the wrestler is to your promotion. Low loyalty means they may talk to rival promotions or threaten to leave.

👑 Ego

How big the wrestler's ego is. High ego wrestlers demand more money, refuse to lose, and cause backstage drama.

📋 Booking Shows

Shows are the core of your promotion. Here's how to book a great card.

Step by Step

  • 1. Select a Venue - Choose based on your budget and expected attendance. Don't book a venue too big or you'll have empty seats.
  • 2. Set Up Production - Choose your ring, lighting, cameras, etc. Better production = better shows but costs more.
  • 3. Add Matches - Book your wrestlers in matches. Consider style chemistry and storylines.
  • 4. Book or Run - Save the show to run later, or run it immediately.

Match Types

Singles Matches

Standard, Hardcore, Cage, Ladder, Tables, Last Man Standing, Iron Man, I Quit, TLC, Hell in a Cell, Inferno, Buried Alive, Deathmatch

All 1 vs 1 format. Different stipulations affect match quality based on wrestler styles.

Multi-Person

Triple Threat (3-way), Fatal Four Way (4-way), Elimination Chamber (6-way)

Every person for themselves. More participants = more chaotic and unpredictable.

Tag Matches

Tag Team (2v2), 6-Man Tag/Trios (3v3), War Games (4v4)

Team-based matches. Good tag chemistry boosts match quality.

Special

Battle Royal (8+), Royal Rumble (8+), Gauntlet (4+)

Large-scale matches for special events. Great for building storylines.

Style Chemistry

Some wrestler style combinations create better matches than others:

  • Giant vs High Flyer = Great chemistry (David vs Goliath)
  • Powerhouse vs Technician = Good chemistry
  • Strong Style vs Strong Style = Excellent chemistry
  • Giant vs Giant = Poor chemistry (too slow)
  • Technician vs Technician = Average (unless in specialty matches like Iron Man)
💡 Tip: Put your best match in the main event slot (last match on the card). Main events get bonus crowd reaction and XP.

🎬 Show Production

Production elements make your show look and feel more professional. They're locked by venue tier.

Production Categories

🟫 Ring

From boxing rings to elite LED rings. Better rings improve match quality and reduce injuries. The 6-sided ring is a unique option that fans love.

💡 Lighting

From house lights to stadium spectaculars. Good lighting dramatically improves the show feel and fan excitement.

📹 Cameras

From phone recording to cinematic production. Better cameras let you create content, build your brand, and attract TV deals.

🚪 Backstage

From no setup to elite production complex. Good backstage keeps wrestlers happy and professional. Includes catering, medical, hair and makeup.

🔥 Pyro

From fog machines to WrestleMania-level displays. Pyro excites fans but costs a lot. Only available in venues with pyro capability.

🚶 Entrance

From curtains to spectacular entrance complexes. Great entrances make wrestlers feel like stars.

🔊 Audio

From bluetooth speakers to stadium sound systems. Good audio makes entrance music hit harder and crowd reactions louder.

💡 Tip: Don't overspend on production early. A boxing ring with house lights is fine for a pub show. Save the spectacular stuff for when you can afford it.

🏆 Championships

Championships give your promotion prestige and your wrestlers something to fight for.

How They Work

  • Unlock Slots - Buy championship slots with money.
  • Create Titles - Choose level (World, Singles, Tag, Trophy), gender division, and rules.
  • Award Champions - Crown your first champion.
  • Defend Titles - Book title matches on your shows. If the challenger wins, the title changes hands automatically.
  • Build Prestige - Regular defenses and great matches build title prestige. Vacating titles loses prestige.

Championship Rules

Standard

Normal rules. Title can be defended in any match type.

Hardcore Only

Title can only be defended in hardcore/deathmatch stipulations.

24/7 Defense

Title can be defended anytime, anywhere. Creates chaos and surprise title changes.

Tournament Only

New champions can only be crowned through tournaments. Creates big moments.

💡 Tip: Don't create too many titles early. One world title is enough to start. Add more as your roster grows.

⭐ Progression & Leveling

Your promotion levels up from 1 to 100. Each level unlocks new features and capabilities.

Key Milestones

Level 1-9: Backyard

Tier 1 venues, 5-12 wrestlers, basic matches only. You're just starting out.

Level 10-19: Local

Tier 2 venues, 15-25 wrestlers, hardcore matches unlocked. Getting known locally.

Level 20-34: Regional

Tier 3 venues (Small Arenas), PPV events, cage and ladder matches. Real promotion status.

Level 35-49: National

Tier 4 venues (Medium Arenas), TV deals, 60+ roster. Known across the country.

Level 50-64: International

Tier 5 venues (Large Arenas like MSG), international touring, Royal Rumble. World stage.

Level 65+: Global & Beyond

Stadiums, super stadiums, unlimited shows. You're one of the biggest promotions in the world.

How to Earn XP

  • Running shows - Base XP for each show, bonus for quality
  • Great matches - 4+ star matches earn bonus XP, 5-star matches earn a lot
  • Sellouts - Filling a venue to capacity earns bonus XP
  • Signing wrestlers - Small XP for each signing
  • Creating championships - XP for establishing titles
  • Weekly passive - Small XP each week based on roster size and fans
  • Achievements - Completing achievements awards XP
💡 Tip: Focus on running consistent quality shows rather than trying to grow too fast. XP adds up over time.

🎭 Creative Control

When enabled, wrestlers have their own personalities and agendas. This creates drama, challenges, and unpredictable moments.

What Can Happen

  • Salary Demands - Wrestlers may demand raises, especially popular ones with big egos.
  • Refusing to Lose - Ego-driven wrestlers may refuse to do the job for someone they see as beneath them.
  • Backstage Confrontations - Wrestlers with grudges may get into arguments or fights.
  • Talking to Rivals - Unhappy or disloyal wrestlers may contact other promotions.
  • Going Into Business - In rare cases, a wrestler may ignore the planned finish and go off-script.
  • Positive Events - Professional wrestlers may mentor young talent or exceed expectations.

Difficulty Levels

😊 Easy

Fewer incidents, more positive events. Good for learning the game.

😐 Normal

Balanced experience. Occasional drama but manageable.

😰 Hard

Frequent incidents, egos run wild. Need good management skills.

🔥 Chaos

Constant drama. Every week is a crisis. For masochists only.

💡 Tip: Keep wrestler morale high by giving them wins, raises, and title opportunities. Happy wrestlers cause fewer problems.

💡 Tips and Tricks

Money Management

  • Start with the cheapest venue and production. Upgrade as you earn.
  • Don't sign too many wrestlers early - salaries add up fast.
  • Focus on ticket sales and merchandise. Bigger crowds = more money.
  • Watch your weekly salary costs. They're paid even if you skip a week.

Building Great Shows

  • Mix match types for variety. Don't book 5 standard matches.
  • Put your best match last (main event gets bonuses).
  • Use style chemistry. Giant vs High Flyer is always exciting.
  • Title matches boost crowd interest and XP.
  • Don't overwork wrestlers. High fatigue leads to bad matches and injuries.

Growing Your Promotion

  • Run shows every week. Skipping weeks loses fans.
  • Invest in production gradually. Each upgrade makes a difference.
  • Create championships to give wrestlers motivation and fans something to care about.
  • Sign a mix of styles for better match variety.
  • Level up to unlock bigger venues and more features.

Managing Your Roster

  • Don't keep wrestlers you're not using. Release them to save salary.
  • Watch contract expirations. Renew key talent early.
  • Balance your roster between men and women for more booking options.
  • Mix rookies (cheap) with veterans (expensive but better) for a balanced budget.
💡 Pro Tip: The best promoters aren't the ones who spend the most - they're the ones who get the most out of what they have. Book smart, not expensive.
